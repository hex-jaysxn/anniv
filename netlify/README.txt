Add your real photos here so they show up permanently (no upload needed):

1. Pick 6 photos of you two.
2. Rename them exactly:  1.jpg  2.jpg  3.jpg  4.jpg  5.jpg  6.jpg
   (if a photo is a .png or .jpeg, convert or rename the extension to .jpg,
   or tell Claude and the code can be adjusted to match)
3. Drop them into this folder (assets/photos/).
4. Open index.html — they'll appear in the scrapbook automatically.

Until you add real files here, each photo slot just shows a soft
placeholder that you can tap to preview a photo from your device.
